About KnowMitra

KnowMitra is a campus peer learning platform designed to connect students who want to share their knowledge and learn new skills from each other. Our platform encourages collaborative learning by allowing students to teach skills they already know and learn skills they are interested in — without paying money.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make learning accessible, collaborative and affordable by creating a community where every student has something valuable to teach and something new to learn. We believe peer-to-peer knowledge exchange is the future of student education.
